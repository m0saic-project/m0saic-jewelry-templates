# Jewelry samples

A handful of real jewelry photos and clips for **Mosaic Desktop**: when someone tries Mosaic for Jewelry or follows
its tour, they have something rooted in reality to drop into the piece's slot before they bring their own. 9 files.
Mosaic Desktop lists this folder first in its Files dock, as "Samples".

All from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial use, no attribution required - credited here
anyway. `samples.json` is the same list for a program: file, kind, size, what it shows, the slot it reads best in, and
the credit. These are deliberately NOT the starter pack (`src/_shared/assets/starter`, what an empty slot shows): drop a
sample into a template and the template visibly changes.
